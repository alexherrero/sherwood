Live mode

{{if .Gate.On}}On: real orders{{else}}Off{{end}}

Sherwood places real orders only when all three of these hold as it starts: the settings file names live mode, you have confirmed live mode here, and the paper record is at least {{.Gate.MinWeeks}} weeks long.

  • The settings file names live mode{{if .Live}}Yes{{else}}No: paper mode{{end}}
  • Confirmed in the appA new strategy file, or a change of mode, needs a new confirmation.{{if .Gate.Confirmed}}Yes, {{.Gate.Confirmed}}{{else}}No{{end}}
  • Paper record long enough{{.Gate.PaperWeeks}} of at least {{.Gate.MinWeeks}} weeks{{if .Gate.PaperMet}}Yes{{else}}No{{end}}
{{if .Gate.On -}}

Live trading is on: orders you approve are placed with the broker and use real money. To stop sending orders at once, turn on the stop switch on Today. To go back to paper trading, set mode: paper in the settings file on the NAS and restart.

{{- else if .Gate.Restart -}}

All three hold. Restart sherwood on the NAS to start live trading; until then nothing is sent to the broker.

{{- else -}}

Live trading is off, so nothing is sent to the broker.{{range .Gate.Reasons}} {{.}}{{end}}
